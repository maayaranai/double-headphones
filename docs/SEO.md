# Double Headphones — Audio Sharing for Windows

## How to play audio through two headphones at the same time on Windows

Double Headphones is a free Windows application that lets you share audio to multiple headphones simultaneously. No drivers needed, no complex setup.

### Common Questions This App Solves

**How do I connect two Bluetooth headphones to my laptop at the same time?**
Double Headphones captures your system audio and routes it to two or more output devices simultaneously — Bluetooth, wired, USB, or any combination.

**Can I play audio on two devices at once on Windows 10 or Windows 11?**
Yes. Double Headphones enables dual audio output on any Windows 10 (build 20348+) or Windows 11 PC without installing virtual audio drivers.

**How to watch a movie with two headphones on one laptop?**
Connect both headphones to your laptop, open Double Headphones, select both devices, and click Share Audio. Both headphones will play the movie audio in sync.

**Is there a free alternative to Voicemeeter for dual headphone output?**
Yes. Double Headphones is free forever, requires no drivers, and is much simpler than Voicemeeter for the specific use case of sharing audio to multiple headphones.

**How to share laptop audio with two Bluetooth earbuds?**
Pair both Bluetooth earbuds with your Windows laptop, launch Double Headphones, select each pair from the dropdown, and click Share Audio.

**Can two people listen to the same audio on one computer?**
Yes. Double Headphones was built exactly for this — two people, two headphones, one laptop, one movie.
