The problem

You're on a train with your partner. You want to watch a movie together on your laptop — but Windows only plays audio through one device at a time. Sharing one pair of earbuds is uncomfortable, splitter cables are never in your bag, and the laptop speaker makes the whole carriage hate you.

The solution

Double Headphones captures your system audio and routes it to two or more output devices at once — Bluetooth, wired, USB, any combination.

  1. Connect both headphones
  2. Select them in the app
  3. Click Share Audio
  4. Both headphones play the same audio, in sync

When you stop, everything returns to normal automatically.

FAQ

How do I play audio through two headphones at once on Windows?

Download the app, connect both headphones, select them, and click Share Audio. Both play in sync. No drivers.

Can I connect two Bluetooth headphones to my laptop?

Yes. Pair both, select each in the app, click Share Audio. If one Bluetooth adapter stutters with two streams, a ~$10 second USB Bluetooth adapter fixes it.

Is this a free Voicemeeter alternative?

Yes — free forever, no drivers, and far simpler for the specific job of sharing audio to two headphones.

Netflix / Disney+ audio is silent

DRM apps block audio capture. Use Chrome instead of the native Windows app — it works perfectly.

Audio is out of sync with video

Bluetooth adds 150–250ms latency. Use the latency slider in the app, or adjust audio delay in your player (VLC: press J/K).
