Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

Double Headphones does not collect any personal data.

Overview

Double Headphones is an audio routing utility that runs entirely on your local computer. Your privacy is important to us, and we have designed this application to operate without collecting, transmitting, or storing any personal information.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information — we do not collect names, emails, or any identifying data
  • No telemetry — we do not track how you use the application
  • No analytics — we do not use any analytics services or tracking pixels
  • No audio data — your audio is processed locally and is never recorded or transmitted
  • No usage statistics — we do not measure feature usage, session duration, or crash data

Local Data Storage

Double Headphones stores a small amount of configuration data locally on your computer to remember your preferences between sessions:

  • Application settings — device selections, volume levels, and preferences stored in %APPDATA%\DoubleHeadphones\config.json
  • Trial information — trial start date and a machine identifier hash stored in %APPDATA%\DoubleHeadphones\trial.json

This data never leaves your computer. You can delete it at any time by removing the %APPDATA%\DoubleHeadphones folder.

Network Connections

Double Headphones does not make any network connections during normal operation. All audio processing happens entirely on your local machine.

The only potential network activity is:

  • Update checks (optional) — the application may check for new versions. This can be disabled in settings. No personal data is sent during update checks.
  • Microsoft Store — if you purchased through the Microsoft Store, Windows may verify your license through standard Store mechanisms.

Audio Processing

Audio signals are routed between devices entirely within your computer's memory. Audio data is:

  • Never recorded to disk
  • Never transmitted over the network
  • Never accessible to any external service
  • Processed in real-time and immediately discarded

Third-Party Services

Double Headphones does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or data brokers. We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any information in the first place.

Children's Privacy

Double Headphones does not collect any data from any users, including children. The application is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this privacy policy and include the changes in the application's release notes. Given that our core principle is to collect no data, we do not anticipate significant changes.
